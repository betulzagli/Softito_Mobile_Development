New NPC's You Can Interact With

1. Rebecca

Rebecca is arriving in the valley, bringing her vibrant energy with her. Bored with her acting career in the city, our young thespian will now be singing at Gus's restaurant on select evenings. She will also stage a theatrical performance during the final week of summer each year. Watching the play will give you an extra boost when trying to impress NPCs during conversations. You'll be able to build your relationships faster than ever!

2. Samantha, Mandy and Paulo

Having lost their home in a fire, these three siblings used the last of their money to take a train to the valley. They are all hardworking and need funds to rebuild their lives. Samantha is an excellent cook, Mandy loves gardening, and Paulo chops wood. If you can't keep up with all the chores on your vast farm, don't hesitate to ask these three siblings for help! Their doors are always open to you—for a fee, of course...

New Map

Would you like to venture into the depths of our new forest, which adds a splash of color to the valley's lively atmosphere? Located just to the right of JojaMart, this forest invites you into its magical world! Rain falls here throughout every season, nurturing a wide variety of fruit trees and other forest flora. With its abundant resources, you’ll no longer have to worry about where to find wood and fruit. And don't forget to look around as you explore—you might just find some gifts waiting for you among the trees!

Gameplay Changes

1. Community Center Update

You no longer need to go to the Community Center building to drop off items you wish to donate. Thanks to a new button we’ve added to your inventory, you can donate the items you find. However, you will still need to visit the Community Center to collect your rewards.

2. Daily Hourly Cycle

We realize that many of you go to great lengths to make it home in time. Sometimes, a single day just isn't enough for all the demanding farm work. That’s why we’ve slowed down the daily time cycle a bit. Now, you’ll be able to get your tasks done without feeling rushed by the clock. Still, try not to get home too late. You know—as Linus says—there are thieves in the valley. Plus, you don't have health insurance...
